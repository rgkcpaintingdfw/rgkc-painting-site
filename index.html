Before we pack up

A straight answer

What paint
won’t fix.

Paint does a lot, but it isn’t magic. We’d rather tell you now than have you find out after we’ve left.

i

It won’t hide bad drywall

A fresh coat makes dents and patches more visible, not less. We point out anything that needs repair first and price it separately.

ii

Big color changes cost more

Going from light to deep navy, or covering a red wall with white, can take a primer and a third coat. That’s on your estimate up front.

iii

Painted brick is close to permanent

It looks great and it lasts, but going back to bare brick is hard and expensive. We talk it through properly before you decide.

iv

Texas summers set the schedule

In July we start at sunrise and follow the shade around the house. Paint applied to a wall baking in direct sun doesn’t cure right.
